Here's what stands out about most astrology apps: too many either read like a generic AI wrote your horoscope, or ease you into an open-ended paid chat with no idea what the final bill will be. Neither approach respects the person asking.

Nakshatra was built to be something gentler. Real sidereal ephemeris data — the same planetary positions a trained astrologer would actually calculate for your janam kundli, never a guess. Real palm reading from a photo. Classical, rule-based readings, with no AI-generated guesswork in between. And one honest price, shown before you pay, with no clock running quietly in the background.

It's still early days for Nakshatra. It's built carefully rather than rushed, with real attention paid to the astronomy, the payment systems, and the data security underneath it. But Nakshatra is live, and real people are already using it.

If you're curious about Vedic astrology, real astronomical calculations, or simply enjoy seeing something meaningful built from scratch — get in touch. This is the kind of project that grows better with company.
