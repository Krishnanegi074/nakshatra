Who we are

Nakshatra ("we", "us", "the app") provides astrology and palmistry content for entertainment and self-reflection. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never stored by us in plain text.
  • Birth details — the date, time (if you provide it), and place of birth you enter, used only to calculate your birth chart.
  • Palm reading answers — your responses to the palmistry questionnaire. If you use the photo-assisted option, the photo is analyzed on your own device only — it is never uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • Content you create — messages sent through the in-app astrologer chat, community posts and likes, and gift messages.
  • Purchase records — which tier you bought or were gifted, and the payment method selected.

How we use this information

  • To calculate and display your birth chart, horoscope, palm reading, compatibility, and other reports.
  • To let you sign back in and see your saved chart and reports on any device.
  • To operate features you choose to use, like gifting a report to someone else or posting to the community feed.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to train AI models — every piece of astrology and palmistry content in this app comes from fixed calculation and content rules, not from an AI reading your personal data.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, behind access controls (Row Level Security) that stop any other app account from reading or writing your rows in the database. Your login session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits.

Third parties

We currently share data with Supabase as part of running the app's backend. If and when real payments are enabled, a payment gateway will process the payment details needed to complete a purchase — we do not handle or store your card or bank details ourselves.

Your choices

  • You can update your birth details at any time by re-entering them.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete My Account. This cannot be undone.
  • You can contact us with any privacy question or request at the address below.

Children's privacy

Nakshatra is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live, to create an account.

How your horoscope is generated

Every word is assembled from a fixed rule library — not written freshly by an AI model each time. Two real, independently verifiable inputs drive it:

1. Your Sun & Moon sign — computed from real planetary ecliptic longitudes at your exact birth moment, using the same open-source astronomical engine (Astronomy Engine) that professional astronomy software relies on.

2. The current real Moon phase — recalculated every time you open the app.

Those two real inputs pick which pre-written template lines are shown to you. Nothing here is generated on the fly by a language model.
